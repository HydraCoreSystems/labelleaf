LabelLeaf privacy

Draft for Phil to review before it goes live.

LabelLeaf has no account, no server, and no cloud storage. Your plant names, your print queue, your calibration offsets and your free label count stay on your phone. The free label count is kept in the iPhone Keychain so that deleting and reinstalling the app does not reset it. It never leaves your phone.

LabelLeaf does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it does not track you or use analytics.

Subscriptions are sold and handled by Apple. Apple tells the app whether a plan is active. LabelLeaf never sees your payment details.

Plant name checks run on your phone against data bundled in the app. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
